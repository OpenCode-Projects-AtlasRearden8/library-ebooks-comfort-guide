# When Things Go Wrong

Digital library loans break in small, boring ways: a book won't open, your page is wrong, the app forgot you exist. The good news — genuinely good — is that almost every failure has a known, quick fix, and your *account* is almost never the thing that's broken. This chapter is a symptom-to-fix field manual. Find your symptom, do the fix, move on.

## The universal first moves (in order)

Whatever the symptom, these four fix most reports:

1. **Check the loan is still active** — Shelf → Loans in Libby; My Hoopla → Currently Borrowed in hoopla; on Kindle, check the loan didn't expire (loans end silently, exactly like a rental).
2. **Update the app.** App stores occasionally hold back Libby/hoopla updates; an outdated app is the most common cause of "it suddenly stopped working." Update, reopen, try again.
3. **Sign out, close fully, sign back in.** Not just backgrounding — actually close the app. This re-fetches your account/list/positions from the server.
4. **Reboot the device.** It's not superstition; networking stacks really do wedge, especially on phones that haven't restarted in weeks.

If those don't clear it, look for your symptom below.

## Symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book vanished from shelf | Loan expired (auto-returned) | Re-borrow; your position is remembered (see note below) |
| Loan says "expired" but you're mid-book | Same as above — clocks run while you sleep | Re-borrow (or renew *before* the next due date; chapter 06) |
| Audiobook won't finish downloading | Wi-Fi drop mid-download; storage full | Reconnect, retry download; free a little storage; restart app |
| Audiobook keeps buffering | Streaming on weak data | Download the title for offline use while on Wi-Fi |
| Ebook opens, then blank/white pages | Reader glitch; oversized fixed-layout title | Close book fully, reopen; if fixed-layout comic/magazine, try the zoom setting; update app |
| "This title can't be opened" message | License dropped off the device | Sign out/in; if present, re-send via Kindle's re-delivery (chapter 04) |
| Reading position went back several pages | Another device on the account read later | They re-sync to whichever position is furthest along; if it persists, try the device-sync fixes below |
| Hold won't let you borrow when it says "ready" | The claim window lapsed or a card issue | Check Holds — if it lapsed, re-attempt; verify card is still active |
| App won't sign in at all | Wrong PIN, expired card, or server issue | Try your PIN variants (chapter 01); check your library's status page/socials; renew card if prompted |
| Book appears on phone but not Kindle | Delivery went to a different device, or Wi-Fi sync pending | Kindle: check Wi-Fi and sync; re-deliver from Amazon Content & Devices (chapter 04) |
| hoopla says "no borrows remaining" | Allowance used up (instant and Flex have separate pools) | Wait for the monthly reset (day varies; check your profile) or return an unneeded borrow early |
| Libby says "you've reached your loan limit" (10-ish titles) | Loans full on that card | Return something early (chapter 06) or borrow on your other card |

## The device-sync section (the real "sync-trouble fixes")

### Libby on two phones/tablets

Libby's account lives on the server; device state syncs down. If your phone and tablet disagree about where you are in the book:

1. Open the book on the *other* device to force a sync pull.
2. If one device is stuck showing an old shelf: **sign out and back in** on that device.
3. Still wrong? Note your *correct* position (chapter, approximate paragraph), then sign out/in on the offender — stale positions reconcile to the server's copy of truth.

Kindle loans add a second sync layer: Libby → Amazon → your device. When a Kindle loan misbehaves, the fix is the **re-delivery** flow in chapter 04 (Amazon's Content & Devices → Books → Deliver/Remove → Make Changes). It re-points the valid loan and resolves stale copies without touching your loan clock.

### hoopla downloads

hoopla offline copies are cached per device. If a download is corrupt (won't play, skips chapters), **delete the offline copy** from the title's page and re-download on Wi-Fi. Accounts sync fine; only the local file is bad.

### Reading on the browser (libbyapp.com / hoopladigital.com)

Browser readers mostly "break" through stale tabs: a tab left open overnight will lose your session. Close the old tab, open a fresh one, sign back in. Progress syncs from the server when you sign in, so nothing is lost.

## When it's genuinely the file

Every once in a while, the license or file itself is bad (publisher delivery error). Symptoms: an ebook that refuses to open *on all devices*, a hold that expired while the book never showed up, an audiobook dead at a specific chapter. The fix is boring:

1. Return the loan early (or let it lapse).
2. Borrow it fresh.
3. If the fresh loan is broken too, **tell your library** — it's reportable, they can flag the title to the platform, and they can usually fix their catalog entry.

## What to say when you call the library (works every time)

Librarians are extremely good at this and *like* fixing it. To make the call five minutes instead of forty, have at hand:

- Your **card number** (not your PIN — don't read it aloud into an office).
- The **device and app version** ("phone, Libby, updated last week").
- The **title/format** and what precisely happens ("opens to a white screen and closes").
- What you already tried (the universal first moves above).

That list answers 90% of the questions they'd otherwise ask one by one. If it's a Kindle-flow issue, mention the account-vs-device sign-in detail from chapter 04; it's the most common root cause they'll check first.

## One habit that prevents most of this

**Borrow, then open the book today.** Most "it broke" reports are really "I borrowed it two weeks ago, never opened it, and the loan expired." Open it once on borrow-day to make sure it downloaded/works, return early if it's not your thing, and the mysterious losses mostly never happen to you.
